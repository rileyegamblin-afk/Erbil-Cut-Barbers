ERBIL CUT — PREMIUM WEBSITE DEMO
=================================

This is a polished sales/demo version designed to show a barber owner what a
finished website could look like BEFORE you receive their real content.

IMPORTANT
---------
Anything marked [REPLACE] is a placeholder. Do not publish the demo as the
official business website until the owner confirms the information.

WHAT TO ASK ERBIL CUT FOR
-------------------------
1. Official logo (PNG/SVG if available)
2. Best phone number
3. Exact opening hours
4. Complete service list + prices
5. Whether they take walk-ins, bookings, or both
6. Booking link, if they use one
7. Instagram/Facebook/TikTok links
8. 5–15 real photos of the shop, barbers and haircuts
9. A short paragraph about the business
10. Any preferred wording, colours or branding
11. Permission to use their photos and supplied business information
12. Confirmation of which customer reviews they want displayed

HOW TO USE
----------
Open index.html locally to preview the site.

For your sales pitch, keep the placeholder version as a DEMO. Once the owner
agrees, replace the placeholders with the real content and photos.

GOOGLE REVIEWS
--------------
The three reviews in this demo came from the information supplied for the
business listing. Before final publication, verify the wording and make sure
the business is happy with the selected testimonials.

NO FAKE PHOTOS
--------------
The dark image panels are intentionally placeholders. Replace them with real
photos from Erbil Cut rather than presenting AI/demo imagery as photographs of
the business.

NO FAKE PRICES
--------------
All prices are [XX] placeholders and must be confirmed.

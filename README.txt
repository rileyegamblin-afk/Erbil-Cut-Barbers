A responsive one-page website concept for Erbil Cut, Taunton.

- Business: Erbil Cut
- Address: 1 E Reach, Taunton TA1 3EN
- Google rating shown in the search result: 5.0 (30 reviews)

## Before giving this to the business
Confirm:
- Phone number
- Exact services and prices
- Whether they accept walk-ins / appointments
- Social media links
- Logo/brand assets
- Photos of the shop and haircuts
- Opening hours

The website deliberately avoids inventing prices or a booking system.
